Sum Insured

charges, surgical  appliances charges
v. Medicines, drugs and consumables
vi. Diagnostic procedures 
vii. The Cost of prosthetic and other Medical devices 
or equipment if implanted internally during a 
Surgical Procedure.

case of admission to a room at rates exceeding 
the aforesaid limits, the reimbursement/payment 
of Room Rent charges including all Associated 
Medical Expenses  incurred at Hospital shall

admissible rate per day bears to the actual rate 
per day of Room Rent charges. This condition 
is not applicable in respect of Hospitals where 
differential billing for Associated Medical 
Expenses is not followed based on Room 
category.

Us, We will apply 10% of Base Sum Insured under 
expiring Cover as Cumulative Bonus in the Coverage  
provided that;
i. There has been no claim under the 
Coverage  in expiring year.
ii. Cumulative Bonus  will be reduced at 
the same rate as accrued in the event of 
admissible Claim under the Coverage.
iii. Cumulative Bonus can be accumulated 
upto the limit mentioned on the Schedule 
of Coverage in the Policy Schedule /
Certificate of Insurance.
iv. Cumulative Bonus  applied will be 
applicable only to Insured Person(s) 
covered under the expiring Coverage  
and who continue to remain insured on 
Renewal.
